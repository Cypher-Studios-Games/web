OPTIONS

Currencies

CLK - Click - Simply represents clicking

TCK - Tick - A reward for playtime

FLO - Flow - The "flow" of the game, allows for lots of trade

CRE - Core - The core of the game, can be used to purchase amplifiers

MNY - Money - A simple cosmetic that will be important in future updates

Upgrades

Generators - Generates clicks

Boosters - Improves generator click-rate

Converters - Converts your CLK into FLO

Amplifiers - Doubles all earnings
