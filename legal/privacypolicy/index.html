Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 10, 2026

Welcome to Cypher Studios. We value your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how we handle your data. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information.

Contact Information:
Cypher Studios

Email: email.cypherstudios@gmail.com

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Account Information: Your email address and a unique User ID (UID) provided by Google Firebase.
  • Profile Data: Your chosen username.
  • Usage Data: We track playtime info and game progress to manage reward systems and improve game balance.
  • Device Information: Technical data such as IP address and browser type, collected automatically via our security and analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your account and save your progress.
  • To process rewards and points earned through third party surveys.
  • To analyze website performance and user engagement via Google Analytics.
  • To protect our service from bots and fraudulent activity.

3. Third Party Service Providers

We share data with trusted partners to operate our site. Each partner has their own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase: Used for account hosting and database management.
  • Google Analytics: Used to understand how users interact with our site.
  • Google reCAPTCHA: This site is protected by reCAPTCHA. Google collects hardware and software information to ensure you are not a bot.
  • Theorem Reach: We use Theorem Reach to provide surveys. When you use their service, they may collect demographic data to match you with surveys.

4. Children's Privacy

Our services are available to users of all ages. For users under the age of 13 in Canada, we recommend parental guidance. We do not knowingly collect or store sensitive personal information from children beyond what is necessary for the operation of the account and reward system.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time. To request a full deletion of your account and all associated playtime data, please contact us at email.cypherstudios@gmail.com.
